# TempFlow — Temperature Converter

## Project Overview

TempFlow is a professional and responsive temperature converter website developed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Web Development & Design Internship — Level 1, Task 3**.

The application allows users to convert temperature values between **Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin**. It also provides educational information about temperature scales, conversion formulas, reference points, and absolute zero.

## Temperature Scales

### Celsius (°C)

Celsius is commonly used around the world for weather and everyday temperature measurements.

- Water freezes at **0°C**
- Water boils at **100°C**

### Fahrenheit (°F)

Fahrenheit is commonly used for everyday temperature measurements in the United States.

- Water freezes at **32°F**
- Water boils at **212°F**

### Kelvin (K)

Kelvin is the scientific SI temperature scale. It begins at absolute zero.

- Absolute zero = **0 K**
- Water freezes at approximately **273.15 K**

## Conversion Formulas

### Celsius to Fahrenheit

```text
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Fahrenheit to Celsius
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
Celsius to Kelvin
K = °C + 273.15
Kelvin to Celsius
°C = K − 273.15
Absolute Zero
Absolute zero is the lowest theoretical temperature.
−273.15°C
−459.67°F
0 K
TempFlow prevents users from entering temperatures below absolute zero and displays a user-friendly validation message.
